# Surface brief: home (página inicial)

Scope: the By Gota home page. Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience: people in Juiz de Fora arriving from Instagram, mostly on a phone. Job: pick cookies and order on WhatsApp, or find the store.
Proof and content: real menu (8 flavors at R$ 14, bundles 4/R$ 54, 5/R$ 65, 6/R$ 77), real photos from the client's stories, real address.
Constraints: no invented prices, reviews, or claims; WhatsApp number and opening hours still to confirm.
Chosen direction: Vitrine (client chose sketch B). Memorable moment: tapping cookies on the shelves fills the bag that sends the order.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is the shop's glass counter display. It refuses the category default of a hero photo over a grid of equal product cards.

OWN-WORLD: Cream wall ground; a dark cocoa display case with caramel wood shelves; cookies stand on the shelves with handwritten paper price tags; heavy tight grotesque headlines in chocolate with a caramel accent word; the Gota script wordmark in the brand's monoline script; butter yellow and coral only as small brand details.

STORY: The visitor sees today's display, believes the cookies are real and generous, taps the ones they want, and sends the order on WhatsApp or walks to Rua Santo Antônio, 765.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top: wordmark left, links right. Huge two-line headline "Cookies da casa." on the left with a short lede on the right. Below, the display case fills the width with the first shelf of four photographed cookies and tags; the bag bar with the WhatsApp action sits at the case's base and stays pinned while the case scrolls.
