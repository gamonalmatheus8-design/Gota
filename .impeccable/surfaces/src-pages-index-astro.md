# Surface brief: home (página inicial)

Scope: the By Gota home page. Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience: people in Juiz de Fora arriving from Instagram, mostly on a phone. Job: pick cookies, build a box, and order on WhatsApp, or find the store.
Proof and content: real menu (8 flavors at R$ 14, bundles 4/R$ 54, 5/R$ 65, 6/R$ 77), real photos from the client's stories, real address.
Constraints: no invented prices, reviews, or claims; WhatsApp number and opening hours still to confirm, and the UI must say so instead of pretending.
Chosen direction: Fornada (client switched from sketch B to sketch A and asked for a fully interactive, impressive front end).
Memorable moment: the flavor's name writes itself in the brand script over the photo, and every cookie you add flies into a box that fills slot by slot.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is today's batch at the Gota kiosk: cookies under the striped awning, ordered at the counter. It refuses the category default of a hero photo over a grid of equal product cards.

OWN-WORLD: The Gota kiosk carried onto the page: a blue and cream striped awning with a scalloped edge under the header, cream panels, the red script "Gota" wordmark, chunky italic display headlines in kiosk red; the menu as a baby-blue striped board (client choice) with red script names and dot leaders; the order as a cream box; chocolate only as text and the dark photo frames; baby blue for stripes, meter and small accents.

STORY: The visitor sees the hero cookie and its name written by hand, flips through flavors, adds cookies that fly into the box, watches the price drop at 4, 5 and 6, and sends the order or walks to Rua Santo Antônio, 765.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Tray rim with script wordmark left, links center, bag count right. On the paper: left column with the address dot, the headline "Gota a gota, feito à mão.", lede with the R$ 14 fact, primary "Montar minha caixa" and secondary "Como chegar". Right column: one large flavor photo with its script name being written, an add button, and a row of flavor chips below it that swaps the photo.
